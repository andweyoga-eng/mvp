About andWeYO^ga

Founded in 2018, andWeYoga has been a sanctuary for wellness seekers and yoga practitioners of all levels. Our mission is to create a welcoming space where everyone can discover the transformative power of yoga.

With certified instructors and a variety of class styles, we're committed to helping you find balance, strength, and inner peace through the ancient practice of yoga.

and We Meet Yogis and Our Story

and Our Story

Our story begins with the transformative power of yoga and the belief that personal healing can create ripples of positive change in the world. What started as one person's journey of recovery has blossomed into a vibrant community.

In April 2023, andWeYoga was born in Bengaluru with a simple yet powerful philosophy: “We meet, we greet, we do whatever we like — we play, we paint, we run, we picnic — and we yoga too.”

Today, andWeYoga continues to grow stronger every day. We've built more than a yoga studio — we've created a family where transformation is celebrated and every individual's journey is honored.
